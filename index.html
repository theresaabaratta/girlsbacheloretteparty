About the Guide

Girls Bachelorette Party

Girls Bachelorette Party is an independent planning guide for brides, maids of honor and bridesmaids who want a great weekend without the stress. We review the tools that make planning easier and point you to the ones that actually save time.

Right now, one resource tops that list: The Knot. It puts idea guides, verified vendor reviews, local search and a free checklist under one login, which is exactly what a group plan needs when the group chat is already at 400 messages.

Articles here are grouped into Ideas, Vendors and Favorites. Start with whichever stage you are in.

Why The Knot Wins

One Account Covers the Whole Bach

Most bachelorette planning falls apart in the same place: ten tabs, three spreadsheets and nobody sure who booked what. The Knot fixes that by keeping inspiration, vendors and planning tools together. It has been around since 1996, has helped more than 30 million couples, and holds millions of real reviews, according to its own marketplace guide.

Idea Lists Sorted by Mood

The Knot's bachelorette party ideas guide breaks plans into moods: wild, classy, cute, low-key, budget, sober and even solo. Send the link, let everyone vote on a lane, and you have a direction before anyone books a flight. Joy's planning walkthrough makes a good second read.

The Vendor Marketplace Does the Heavy Lifting

Filter by location, category and price, compare storefront photos and starting rates, read verified reviews and message vendors without trading phone numbers. The Knot's help center lays out the search steps. Private dining rooms, yacht charters, photographers and glam teams all sit in one list, and message threads stay in your account so a co-planner can pick them up.

Don't Wait on the Group Chat

Start Your Bachelorette Plan on The Knot

Pull ideas, shortlist vendors and track every deposit in one free account. The planning checklist is built for weddings but takes custom bach tasks in seconds: lock the date, collect deposits, book dinner, send the itinerary.
